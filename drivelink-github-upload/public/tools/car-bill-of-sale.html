Before you sign

  • It doesn't replace the title. The seller still signs the title over, and the mileage disclosure is usually made on the title itself. See how to transfer a car title.
  • Some states have their own form or require a notary. Check your state's motor vehicle agency. In New Jersey, see our New Jersey bill of sale guide.
  • Print two copies and both sign both, so each of you keeps an original.
  • Sign only once the payment is real. Our guides on selling and buying privately cover safe ways to pay.

Where DriveLink fits

A bill of sale records the deal. DriveLink protects the money: the buyer pays through DriveLink, and the seller can't withdraw it until the buyer confirms they have the car and title. It works on a sale you arranged on Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist or anywhere else.

Antes de firmar

  • Imprime dos copias y que ambos firmen las dos, para que cada uno tenga un original.
