What to do if it looks wrong

  • Stop sending money, codes or personal details.
  • Search the seller's photos and the VIN to see if they appear in other listings.
  • Report the listing to the platform you found it on.
  • If you already sent money, contact your bank right away and report it at ic3.gov.

Read how these scams work in our guides on Facebook Marketplace scams, Craigslist scams and wire transfer scams.

Where DriveLink fits
